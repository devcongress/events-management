# Separate Calls for Speakers

## Product boundary

Monthly meetups and the Annual Conference share the submission engine, but retain distinct public calls, review queues, URLs, communications, and organizer destinations.

## Completion checklist

- [ ] Monthly public call and review remain event-scoped.
- [ ] Annual Conference public call and review remain edition-scoped.
- [ ] Initial form collects only name, email, title, and summary.
- [ ] Accepted speakers receive the existing secure follow-up path for deferred details.
- [ ] Public routes, emails, Slack notifications, audit records, and permissions identify the call scope.
